The Blind Spot

Emotion AI has a geography problem

Every emotion recognition system deployed at scale today was conceived in a Western lab, trained on Western faces and validated against Western standards of emotional expression. When a Sri Lankan laughs, it gets categorised. When they mourn, it gets misread. And when they express something with no Western equivalent - the algorithm just shrugs.

For Sri Lanka's bilingual generation - toggling between Sinhala and English in a single sentence - this isn't an edge case. It's the entire experience.

The Architecture

One App. Four Engines.
Zero Compromises.

Not a single overcrowded model trying to do everything - four precision-built deep learning specialists, each owning one domain of Sri Lankan emotion intelligence, unified inside a single seamless mobile experience.

ENGINE · 01

Regional Facial Emotion Recognition

A deep learning model - the first to actually capture Sri Lankan facial emotion expressions correctly. It identifies culturally specific emotional variations unique to Sri Lankans, directly addressing the demographic bias embedded in every existing Emotion AI system.

Deep Learning CNN Cultural Equity
ENGINE · 02

Hashtag-Guided Content Recommendation

A deep learning model - the first to generate emotion-aware hashtags in both English and Sinhala, derived directly from the facial expressions detected in a given image. Your face tells the story and the engine writes the caption.

Deep Learning Bilingual NLP Recommender
ENGINE · 03

Multilingual Text & Emoji Emotion Detector

A deep learning model that detects emotions embedded in Singlish text - the natural English-Sinhala code-switching Sri Lankans use every day - including the emoji layer that carries its own distinct emotional meaning alongside the words.

Deep Learning Singlish NLP Emoji Semantics
ENGINE · 04

Speech Emotion Valency Recognition

A deep learning model that identifies emotional valency - positive or negative - from a given Sinhala audio clip. It reads the acoustic fingerprints unique to Sri Lankan speech patterns, going far beyond what any transcription or text analysis alone could ever surface.

Deep Learning Sinhala Audio Valency Detection
